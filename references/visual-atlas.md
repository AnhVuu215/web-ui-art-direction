## Synthesis for future work

1. **Authorship comes from a reasoned system.** The strongest examples repeat a limited motif or material, while giving each section a different role and pace.
2. **Human presence is contextual.** A worker on a real lawn, a designer's actual portrait, a travel scene, or a hand using a device conveys more than generic “human” stock art when it matches the offer.
3. **Many images prioritize presentation over use.** Tiny annotations, pale type, motion-dependent galleries, and unverified claims are frequent. Borrow the art direction; rebuild the interaction for the actual product.
4. **No single style wins.** Editorial white, immersive black, tactile orange, botanical softness, and hard grid systems all appear. Choose by audience, task, content, and brand, not by frequency in this set.
